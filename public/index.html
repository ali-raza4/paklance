Questions people ask

Good to know

Does it cost anything to post a job?

No. Posting a job, receiving proposals and getting a Match™ shortlist are free. A small service fee applies only when you fund a milestone, and it’s shown before you pay. See pricing.

When does the specialist get paid?

As soon as the client approves the milestone. The money is already held by SafePay™, so it’s released straight away.

What if the work isn’t delivered?

The client can ask for changes. If you can’t agree, either side opens a case, and the funded money stays protected until it’s resolved. How protection works.

Can international companies hire through Paklance?

Yes. Many specialists work hours that overlap with UK, European and Gulf teams. Check your overlap.

How do specialists withdraw their money?

To a Pakistani bank account or a Raast ID. JazzCash and Easypaisa are coming soon.
